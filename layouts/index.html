What is Hexzzle?

Place color. Connect color. Bloom clear.

Hexzzle is a Honeycomb Color Puzzle built for quick browser play. Drop colorful Hive Pieces onto a 37-cell board, connect 6 or more matching colors, and clear space with a gentle Bloom Clear.

How to play

Choose a piece, tap an empty spot, and build connected color groups. Scattered colors do not clear, so shape matters.

Why it is fun

Every Bloom Clear opens the board in a new way. The puzzle stays calm, but the tray keeps asking for tiny choices.

Play on mobile or desktop

The board, tray, and controls are sized for short sessions on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Share your Bloom score

After game over, share your score, total Blooms, best chain, and best group without logging in.
