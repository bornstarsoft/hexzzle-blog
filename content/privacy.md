# Privacy

Hexzzle is designed as a lightweight browser game.

Classic mode stores your best score and sound preference locally in your browser. This MVP does not add login, payments, coins, gems, forced ads, rewarded ads, or unnecessary third-party tracking.

If analytics are added later, they should be minimal, privacy-conscious, and documented here before launch.
