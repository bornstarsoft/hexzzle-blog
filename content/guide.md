# How to Play Hexzzle

1. Place pieces on the honeycomb board.
2. Connect matching colors.
3. Make 6 or more connected colors bloom.
4. Scattered colors do not count.
5. Keep space open.
6. Use Undo wisely.
7. Share your result after the game.

Hexzzle is built for short sessions. There is no timer pressure, no login, and no reward for sharing.
