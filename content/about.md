# About Hexzzle

Hexzzle is a free Color Hive Puzzle for browsers. Place Hive Pieces on a Honeycomb Board, stack matching colors, and reach 6 to make the hive bloom.

The game is designed to feel calm, quick to restart, and easy to understand on mobile or desktop.
