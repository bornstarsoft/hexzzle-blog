# About Hexzzle

Hexzzle is a free Color Hive Puzzle for browsers. Place Hive Pieces on a Honeycomb Board, connect matching colors, and make groups bloom to clear space.

The game is designed to feel calm, quick to restart, and easy to understand on mobile or desktop.
