## How to Play

Place colorful pieces on the honeycomb board. Stack matching colors and reach 6 to make the hive bloom.

## Tips

- Keep the middle open when you can.
- Use small Hive Pieces to finish Bloom Stacks.
- Watch stack counts and aim for 6.
- A Bloom Clear can open space for the next tray.
- Undo is best saved for crowded boards.
