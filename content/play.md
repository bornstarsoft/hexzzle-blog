## How to Play

Place colorful pieces on the honeycomb board. Connect 6 or more matching colors to make them bloom.

## Tips

- Keep the middle open when you can.
- Use small Hive Pieces to connect groups.
- A Bloom Clear can open space for the next tray.
- Undo is best saved for crowded boards.
