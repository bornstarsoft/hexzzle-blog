Nintendo has made a significant move for retro gaming enthusiasts by adding five new Virtual Boy titles to its Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack service. This update brings the total number of available games to 12 in the West, with classics like V-Tetris and Space Invaders Virtual Collection now accessible to players. With only 22 games ever released for the Virtual Boy, this means that gamers are just a few titles away from experiencing the majority of this unique library.

The addition of these games not only highlights Nintendo's commitment to preserving its gaming history but also offers a nostalgic trip for fans of the original Virtual Boy console. As the company continues to roll out more titles, including the highly anticipated Mario Clash and unreleased gems like Zero Racer, it raises the question: how will these retro experiences shape the future of gaming on modern platforms?
